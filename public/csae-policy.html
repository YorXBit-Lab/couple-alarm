🛡️ CSAE Policy

Public Standards for Child Safety

Couple Alarm's Commitment

Couple Alarm is committed to protecting children from all forms of sexual abuse and exploitation (Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation - CSAE). We strictly comply with international and local laws regarding child protection, including but not limited to:

  • Vietnam Law on Child Protection (2016, amended 2024)
  • Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC)
  • US Federal Law 18 U.S.C. § 2251-2260
  • EU Directive 2011/93/EU
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Age Requirements and Parental Consent

⚠️ Age Requirements:

  • 13+ years: Users aged 13-17 must have parental consent to use Couple Alarm
  • Under 13: Not permitted to use the service in compliance with COPPA
  • 18+ years: Full access without restrictions

Couple Alarm is designed for managing notes, tasks, and reminders between partners. The app contains only productivity features without any adult content. However, we require parental oversight for minors to ensure safe and appropriate usage.

Safe and Appropriate Use

Couple Alarm is a family-friendly productivity app designed for:

  • Sharing reminders for important dates and events
  • Collaborative note-taking and memory keeping
  • Managing shared to-do lists and tasks
  • Organizing daily activities between partners

Our app does not contain, promote, or facilitate any inappropriate content. All features are designed for legitimate productivity and relationship management purposes.

Absolutely Prohibited Content

Couple Alarm strictly prohibits all CSAE-related content, including:

  • Images, videos, or text depicting or suggesting child sexual abuse
  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Grooming or approaching minors with malicious intent
  • Trading, buying, selling, or exchanging CSAE content
  • Any form of child sexual exploitation or abuse
  • Content that encourages, glorifies, or legitimizes CSAE
  • Sexually explicit content involving or targeting minors
  • Content that sexualizes children in any way

Monitoring and Detection Mechanisms

1. Content Moderation

While Couple Alarm respects user privacy with end-to-end encryption features, we implement multiple layers of protection:

  • Automated scanning of metadata and file properties
  • AI-powered detection of suspicious patterns and behaviors
  • Hash-matching against known CSAM databases (PhotoDNA, etc.)
  • Manual review of reported accounts and content
  • Age verification mechanisms for account creation

2. Reporting System

Users can report suspicious content or behavior through multiple channels:

  • In-app "Report" button (available on all content)
  • Email: YOrXBit@gmail.com
  • Anonymous reporting option for sensitive cases

All reports are reviewed within 24 hours, with priority given to child safety concerns.

Violation Handling

When CSAE content is detected, we will:

  • 🚫 Immediately and permanently suspend the violating account
  • 🗑️ Delete all violating content from our systems
  • 👮 Report to law enforcement (Local Police, FBI, Interpol, NCMEC)
  • 🔒 Preserve evidence as required by law enforcement
  • 🌐 Share information with international anti-CSAE organizations
  • ⚠️ Ban associated accounts and prevent re-registration

Zero Tolerance: We have a zero-tolerance policy for CSAE content. Any violation will result in immediate account termination and legal reporting.

Cooperation with Authorities

Couple Alarm maintains active partnerships with:

  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) - CyberTipline reporting
  • Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) - CSAM hash database
  • Cyber Crime Prevention Department - Vietnam Police
  • INTERPOL - International Crimes Against Children unit
  • FBI Cyber Division - Child Exploitation and Obscenity Section
  • Technology Coalition - Industry collaboration against CSAE

We respond to law enforcement requests within required timeframes and provide full cooperation in investigations involving child safety.

Parental Controls and Guidance

For parents of users aged 13-17, we recommend:

  • Discussing appropriate use of the app with your child
  • Monitoring your child's app usage periodically
  • Teaching your child about online safety and reporting
  • Being aware of who your child connects with on the app
  • Reporting any suspicious behavior immediately

Parents can contact us at YOrXBit@gmail.com for guidance on supervising their child's use of Couple Alarm.

User Responsibilities

All Couple Alarm users are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate age information during registration
  • Not uploading, sharing, or storing any CSAE content
  • Reporting immediately when detecting suspicious content or behavior
  • Not using the app to contact or communicate with minors inappropriately
  • Not using the app for any illegal purposes
  • Complying with all applicable child protection laws
  • Respecting other users' privacy and safety

Education and Awareness

We are committed to raising community awareness about CSAE through:

  • Providing safety educational materials within the app
  • Partnering with child protection organizations
  • Organizing awareness campaigns and initiatives
  • Training our staff on identifying and handling CSAE
  • Publishing regular updates on child safety measures
  • Supporting research and prevention programs

Privacy and Data Protection for Minors

For users under 18, we take additional precautions:

  • Limited data collection in compliance with COPPA
  • No behavioral advertising targeted at minors
  • Enhanced privacy settings by default
  • Parental rights to access and delete their child's data
  • No sharing of minor's personal information with third parties
